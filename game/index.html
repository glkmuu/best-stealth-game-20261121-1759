GHOSTLIGHT HEIST

A museum at night. Three vaults. One thief who was never there.

Sneak past the guards, steal every artifact, reach the exit unseen. Vision cones are real — walls and crates block sight. Light pools and running get you spotted faster. Get caught three times and the night is over.

WASD / arrows — move  ·  Shift — sneak  ·  Space — run (loud!)  ·  E — steal  ·  H — hint  ·  R — restart level  ·  P — pause  ·  M — mute

HOW TO PLAY

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Shift — sneak. Slow and quiet, and guards spot you much slower.
  • Space — run. Fast, but loud: the noise ring draws guards, and the dog hears it.
  • E — steal an artifact when you stand next to it. Steal them all to unlock the exit.
  • Vision cones are yellow. Stay out of them, or break line of sight behind crates, shelves and pillars.
  • Light pools raise detection. Cameras sweep their own cones — one full sweep and the alarm sounds.
  • Detection: the eye fills as a guard sees you. At half, guards come to look. At full, they chase.
  • H — hint points an arrow at the exit (3 per level). R restarts the level. P pauses.
  • Finish a level fast and never detected for the ghost bonus and 3 stars.
